# Lesson text audit — October 15, 2026

Reviewed all 96 teaching phrases and 48 roleplay prompts for Chinese wording, simplified/traditional correspondence, pinyin, English meaning, usage notes, and response context. This audit covers written lesson content; it does not independently evaluate the Mac speech voice's recordings.

## The daily phrase

我确认一下，你是说……

**Wǒ quèrèn yíxià, nǐ shì shuō…**

The existing **yíxià** is correct for connected spoken Mandarin. 一 has the base tone **yī**, but before fourth-tone 下 **xià**, it becomes rising **yí**. A dictionary may write **yīxià** to preserve the base tone. The app uses the spoken change for 一 and 不.

[MIT's Mandarin pronunciation guide](https://web.mit.edu/jinzhang/www/pinyin/tones/) explains the changes for 一 and 不, the distinction between speech and written third-tone marks, and neutral tones. [Mango Languages' lesson on small quantities](https://www.mangolanguages.com/resources/learn/grammar/mandarin-chinese/how-to-use-small-quantity-words-in-mandarin-chinese) also gives 一下 as **yíxià**. [ChinesePod's dictionary](https://www.chinesepod.com/dictionary/%E4%B8%80%E4%B8%8B) illustrates the base-tone spelling **yī xià**.

## Notation used throughout the curriculum

- Show the usual spoken changes of 一: **yíxià**, **yí biàn**, **yí kuài**; **yìdiǎn**, **yìqǐ**, **yì jiā**, **yì bēi**, **yìzhí**.
- Show rising 不 before a fourth tone: **búcuò**, **bú gòu**, **búguò**. Preserve **bù** in 不好意思 and 不着急.
- Use unmarked syllables for common conversational neutral tones, including **bu** in 要不要, 辣不辣, and the potential complement 吃不完.
- Preserve lexical third-tone marks in writing, including **nǐ hǎo**, **kěyǐ**, and **xiǎngfǎ**. Written pinyin is not a transcription of every context-dependent pitch movement.
- Use common mainland conversational pronunciation. Choosing traditional characters changes the script; it does not switch the curriculum to Taiwanese Mandarin. Neutral-tone patterns can differ by region and emphasis.

## Friendly tech-team register

Work phrases and partner dialogue now target quick conversations with familiar coworkers and managers at an informal tech company. Feature updates, getting stuck on a task, code changes, quick reviews, and small experiments provide the context. Considerate requests and simple thanks retain politeness.

| Situation | Current phrase | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Check availability | 你现在有空吗？ | Do you have a moment? |
| Share a blocker | 我这边有个地方卡住了。 | I'm stuck on something on my end. |
| Check a plan | 你看这样可以吗？ | Does that work for you? |
| Clarify a reference | 你是说哪一块？ | Which part do you mean? |
| Schedule a chat | 我们找个时间聊聊吧。 | Let's find a time to chat. |
| Follow up | 上次说的那个事，现在怎么样了？ | Any updates on what we talked about last time? |

The update keeps ordinary polite phrases that already fit, such as **不好意思，你能再说一遍吗？** and **能帮我看一下这个吗？**. Lesson notes explain how 一下, questions, and acknowledging the other person's time help these short sentences stay considerate. Phrase IDs remain stable so saved phrases and progress still point to the same learning topics.

[ChinesePod's asking-for-help lesson](https://chinesepod.com/lessons/asking-for-help) uses 你现在有空吗？ in an office context. Its dictionary gives [卡住 as kǎ zhù, with qiǎ zhù as another pronunciation](https://www.chinesepod.com/dictionary/%E5%8D%A1%E4%BD%8F).

## Everyday-life expansion

Eight new lessons add 48 phrases and 24 roleplay turns. They cover talking about your day, making plans, changing plans, discussing a show, shopping, cooking, visiting friends, and asking for small practical help. Each dialogue has a concrete speaking goal and scene-specific response choices.

The new phrases keep the friendly register, explicit usage notes, and matched simplified/traditional text. Script selection preserves the same words and pinyin; regional alternatives are explained in notes instead of silently changing the wording. New tone-change examples include 一天 **yì tiān**, 一部 **yí bù**, 这么一说 **zhème yì shuō**, 大一号 **dà yí hào**. 连不上 uses conversational neutral **bu** in a potential complement.

Usage references include [ChinesePod’s visiting lesson](https://www.chinesepod.com/lesson/do-i-need-to-remove-my-shoes) for asking about changing shoes and [its dictionary entry for 消息](https://www.chinesepod.com/dictionary/%E6%B6%88%E6%81%AF) for neutral **xiāoxi**. The pinyin tests cover the additional 一 examples and neutral-tone patterns. All 16 lessons are exercised through their full learning and roleplay flow.

## Regression checks

Automated checks protect the daily phrase and all current 一/不 examples, verify selected neutral-tone conventions, require a speaking goal for every roleplay turn, and exercise the new explanations in the renderer. The complete suite has 16 passing tests. These tests protect reviewed choices from accidental edits; they are not an automatic language-accuracy judge.
